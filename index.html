Rethinking AI Beyond the Hype

George Popescu AI is a space for clear, evidence-based thinking about artificial intelligence. George Popescu takes the AI boom seriously—but questions whether ever-larger models, data centers, and valuations are truly moving us toward real intelligence, creativity, or progress.

Ideas That Set This Perspective Apart

George Popescu AI separates signal from noise in today's AI boom. Rather than betting on ever-larger language models, this site explores AI as a powerful human–computer interface and argues that real long-term progress will come from embodied AI—systems that perceive, move, and act in the physical world.

Why Embodied AI Matters

  • Grounded in physics, not just data

Key Themes in George's AI Thinking

This site curates essays, talks, and notes on three core questions: Is today's AI boom sustainable? What are the limits of language-only systems? And how do embodied AI and humanoid robots unlock value that pure software cannot? George approaches these topics as an investor, builder, and skeptic who still wants technology to win in the long run.

A Critical Yet Optimistic View on AI

At George Popescu AI, we look beyond marketing slogans and stock-market narratives. The goal is not to dismiss AI, but to ask harder questions about where it actually creates value—and where it is simply shifting risk and leverage around the system.

We examine the gap between investment and real-world returns, the limits of language-only systems, and the structural risks of an AI-driven debt and infrastructure boom. The aim is a calm, clear, and honest conversation rather than hype or doom.

Despite the noise, there is real reason for optimism—especially where AI meets embodiment: robots, automation, and systems that solve messy physical problems. This is where George believes the next durable wave of progress will come from, and where his thinking connects AI with his work in humanoid robotics.
